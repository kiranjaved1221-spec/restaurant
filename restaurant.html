Our Story

Dis Ole House Kitchen & Garden Barbados preserves authentic Bajan culinary traditions through slow-cooked Caribbean recipes, welcoming hospitality, and beautiful outdoor dining. Rooted in Long Bay, Saint Philip, we bring you heirloom flavors passed down for generations.

Mission & Values

Celebrating Bajan heritage, sustainable seafood, and creating warm memories. Our garden is an extension of our kitchen – fresh herbs, local produce, and the rhythm of island life.

Caribbean Heritage

From fish fry to coconut bread, every dish tells a story of Barbados. We honor the traditions of our grandmothers and the bounty of our island.
